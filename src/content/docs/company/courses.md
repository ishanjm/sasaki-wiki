At **Sasaki Nihon Academy**, our curriculum is tailored to equip students with both practical conversational ability and recognized qualifications required for immigration, academic enrolment, and employment in Japan.

---

## Course Offerings

### 1. JLPT Preparation (N5 to N2)
- **JLPT N5 (Beginner Level)**: Basic grammar, Hiragana, Katakana, ~100 Kanji, everyday vocabulary, and simple everyday conversations. Required for initial student visa and technical trainee entry.
- **JLPT N4 (Elementary Level)**: Understanding everyday Japanese topics, listening comprehension, ~300 Kanji, and foundation for Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visas.
- **JLPT N3 / N2 (Intermediate to Advanced)**: Comprehensive preparation for white-collar employment, university admissions, and specialized technical roles in Japan.

### 2. JFT-Basic (Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese)
- Fast-track exam prep focused on practical communication skills needed for daily life and work in Japan.
- Specifically aligned with the requirements for the **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能)** visa category (A2 level).

### 3. NAT-TEST Preparation
- Accredited examination aligned with JLPT criteria, frequently scheduled throughout the year to speed up student visa application timelines.

---

## Training Methodology

- **Holistic Skill Development**: Balanced focus across Listening (Choukai), Reading (Dokkai), Grammar (Bunpou), and Vocabulary/Kanji (Mojigoi).
- **Japanese Cultural Immersion**: Training in business manners (*Aisatsu*, *Housouren*), workplace etiquette, and daily life norms in Japan.
- **Interactive Practice**: Native-level audio resources, speaking workshops, and simulated interview sessions with Japanese representatives.
- **Individualized Attention**: Small batch sizes to ensure each learner achieves testing proficiency.
