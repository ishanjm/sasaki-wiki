**Sasaki Nihon Academy (Pvt) Ltd** is a premier Japanese language institution and visa consultancy based in Sri Lanka, partnered with **Sasaki Foreign Employment Agency**. Dedicated to bridging ambitious talent with world-class education and employment opportunities in Japan, the academy provides authentic linguistic instruction, professional visa facilitation, and end-to-end relocation support.

Official Website: [www.sasakinihon.lk](https://www.sasakinihon.lk)

---

## Vision & Mission

### Our Vision
To become the most trusted gateway for students and professionals seeking educational and career opportunities in Japan.

### Our Mission
To provide reliable guidance, professional visa support, and quality consultancy services that empower individuals to achieve their dreams in Japan.

## Key Pillars of Service

1. **Academic Excellence & Language Proficiency**: Specialized instruction preparing candidates for JLPT (N5 to N2), NAT-TEST, and JFT-Basic examinations.
2. **Student Visa Assistance**: Partnering with esteemed Japanese language schools, vocational colleges, and universities across Japan.
3. **Work Visa & Career Pathways**: Professional guidance for Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能) and Technical Intern Training (TITP) programs.
4. **Comprehensive Welfare & Post-Arrival Care**: Continuous liaison with Japanese supervising entities, host companies, and candidate families to guarantee welfare and peace of mind.
