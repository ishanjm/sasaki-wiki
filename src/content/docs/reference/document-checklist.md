---
title: Required Documents Checklist (COE & Embassy)
description: Master checklist of certificates, financial records, translations, and forms for Japan visa applications.
---

Proper documentation is the single most critical factor in securing Certificate of Eligibility (COE) clearance and avoiding visa rejections. All non-English documents must be accompanied by certified English or Japanese translations.

---

## 1. Applicant's Personal Documents

| Document | Specifications | Student | SSW / TITP |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Current Passport** | Valid for at least 1 year from intended travel date; original + colour copies of bio-data and visa pages | Required | Required |
| **Passport-Sized Photographs** | 3.0 cm × 4.0 cm, plain white background, taken within last 3 months, matte or semi-gloss finish | 6 Copies | 6 Copies |
| **Birth Certificate** | Certified original copy issued within last 6 months + sworn English translation authenticated by Dept. of Registrar General | Required | Required |
| **National Identity Card (NIC)** | Clear colour copy front and back + sworn English translation | Required | Required |
| **Police Clearance Certificate** | Issued by Sri Lanka Police Headquarters; valid for 6 months | Recommended | Required |
| **Medical Examination Report** | Certified report from designated diagnostic clinic including Chest X-ray, Blood tests, ECG | Required | Required |

## 2. Educational & Language Qualifications

| Document | Specifications | Student | SSW / TITP |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **G.C.E. O/L Certificate** | Certified copy issued by Department of Examinations, Battaramulla | Required | Required |
| **G.C.E. A/L Certificate** | Certified copy showing pass grades in at least 3 subjects | Required | If applicable |
| **Japanese Language Certificate** | Official original score sheet and certificate for **JLPT** (N5/N4), **NAT-TEST**, or **JFT-Basic** | Required | Required |
| **Study Hours Certificate** | Official letter certifying 150+ hours of classroom instruction issued by Sasaki Foreign Employment Agency | Required | Optional |
| **Vocational / Degree Certificates** | University degree, NVQ certificate, or diploma transcript (with English translation) | If applicable | If applicable |
| **Industry Skill Test Certificate** | Official pass certificate for the Prometric SSW Skill Evaluation Exam in the chosen sector | N/A | Required |

---

## 3. Financial Sponsor Documents (Mainly for Student Visas)

| Document | Specifications | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Letter of Financial Sponsorship** | Prescribed form signed by sponsor affirming full responsibility for tuition and living costs | Proves financial commitment |
| **Bank Balance Certificate** | Original certificate from an accredited commercial bank verifying funds (equivalent to **2.5 – 3.0 Million JPY**) | Verifies immediate liquid capital |
| **Bank Statements (1 to 3 Years)** | Certified continuous transaction history showing steady balances and source of deposits | Verifies transaction authenticity |
| **Proof of Income / Salary Slip** | Official pay slips (last 12 months) and employment verification letter stating designation, join date, and annual income | Proves ongoing earning capacity |
| **Business Registration (BR)** | If sponsor is self-employed or business owner: Certified copy of Business Registration + tax receipts | Verifies business legitimacy |
| **Income Tax Returns (3 Years)** | Receipts / assessment notices issued by the Department of Inland Revenue | Establishes declared taxable income |
| **Proof of Relationship** | Birth certificate of student and sponsor showing family linkage (e.g., parent-child relationship) | Confirms authentic sponsor connection |

---

## 4. Japanese Side Documents (Issued by Host Institution)

- **Certificate of Eligibility (COE / 在留資格認定証明書)**: Issued by the regional Japanese Immigration Services Agency.
- **Letter of Acceptance / Enrollment Permit** (for students) or **Binding Employment Contract & Working Conditions Notice** (for workers).
- **Institution Profile**: Copy of company registration (*Tokibo Tohon*) or educational institution license.
- **Support Plan & Fee Schedule**: Clear breakdown of tuition or salary schedules.

---

## 5. Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) Requirements

For all employment visas (SSW, TITP, Engineering):
1. **SLBFE Registration Clearance**: Registration card and biometric data recording.
2. **SLBFE Insurance Policy**: Mandatory insurance coverage protecting worker and family against disability or repatriation costs.
3. **Signed Bilateral Agreement**: Attested by the SLBFE prior to departure.
