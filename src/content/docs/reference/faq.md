## General & Academic Questions

### Q1: I don't know any Japanese. Can I still join your academy?
**Yes.** All our courses start from absolute zero (Hiragana and Katakana alphabets). Our structured curriculum takes you step-by-step from beginner level to examination readiness.

### Q2: What is the difference between JLPT and JFT-Basic? Which should I take?
- **JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test)**: Conducted only twice a year (July and December). Highly respected globally and accepted for all visa types.
- **JFT-Basic (Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese)**: Computer-based test held multiple times throughout the year in Colombo. You receive immediate score results on test day! Perfect for candidates who want to apply for **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)** work visas quickly without waiting 6 months for JLPT results.

### Q3: What is the upper age limit for applying?
- **Student Visa**: Best suited for ages 18 to 30 (candidates older than 30 must show a strong study reason and career progression).
- **SSW Work Visa**: Open to candidates aged 18 to 40+ as long as you pass the language and skill examinations and meet health requirements.
- **TITP**: Typically ages 18 to 35.

## Financial & Visa Questions

### Q4: For a Student Visa, does the financial sponsor need to deposit actual cash in Japan?
**No.** The funds (approx. 2.5 to 3.0 Million JPY, equivalent to ~6–7 Million LKR) remain in your sponsor's commercial bank account in Sri Lanka. Japanese immigration only requires proof of funds and legitimate accumulation history through bank statements and tax records.

### Q5: Can I change my job in Japan if I am on an SSW visa?
**Yes.** Unlike the legacy trainee system, workers under the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) category have the legal freedom to change employers within the same industrial sector across Japan.

### Q6: Can I send money back to my family in Sri Lanka?
**Yes.** Japan has multiple authorized, fast remittance services (Kyodai Remittance, SBI Remit, Western Union) offering mobile apps. You can remit funds directly into any Sri Lankan bank account in LKR within minutes.

### Q7: Can my spouse and children come with me to Japan?
- **Engineer / Specialist in Humanities**: **Yes**, your spouse and children can join you immediately on Dependent visas.
- **SSW Level 1 & TITP**: No family accompaniment allowed during the initial period.
- **SSW Level 2**: **Yes**, family accompaniment is fully permitted.

### Q8: What happens to my pension contributions when I return to Sri Lanka?
Under the Japanese Social Insurance Law, departing foreign workers can claim the **Lump-Sum Withdrawal Payment (*Dattai Ichijikin*)**, receiving a major portion of their accumulated pension funds transferred directly to their Sri Lankan bank account!
