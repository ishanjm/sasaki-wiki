## Candidate Journey to Japan

Follow the structured pathway from initial enrollment to successful deployment overseas:

<Steps>
1. **Registration & Eligibility Assessment**
   Submit academic and professional credentials, complete an initial interview, and verify medical and age eligibility.

2. **Language Training & Skill Certification**
   Enroll in intensive Japanese language training (JLPT / JFT-Basic) and industry-specific skill test preparation at Nihon Academy.

3. **Employer Interview & Contract Signing**
   Participate in direct employer interviews (online or in-person). Selected candidates receive verified, legally compliant employment contracts.

4. **COE & Visa Processing**
   Our documentation team coordinates Certificate of Eligibility (COE) issuance with Japanese immigration and finalizes the embassy work visa.

5. **Pre-Departure Briefing & Overseas Deployment**
   Complete official foreign employment bureau registration, pre-departure orientation, travel coordination, and reception in Japan.
</Steps>
