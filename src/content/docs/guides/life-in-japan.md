## 1. Estimated Monthly Living Budget in Japan

While costs vary between central Tokyo and regional prefectures (e.g., Osaka, Nagoya, Fukuoka, Gunma), here is a realistic monthly budget breakdown:

| Expense Item | Regional Prefecture (Yen) | Tokyo / Urban Center (Yen) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rent / Housing** | ¥25,000 – ¥45,000 | ¥55,000 – ¥75,000 | Employer dorms or shared apartments (*Aパート*) |
| **Utilities (Electricity, Water, Gas)** | ¥8,000 – ¥12,000 | ¥10,000 – ¥14,000 | Higher during winter (heating) & summer (A/C) |
| **Food & Groceries** | ¥25,000 – ¥35,000 | ¥30,000 – ¥40,000 | Cooking at home saves up to 50% vs eating out |
| **Mobile SIM / High-Speed Internet** | ¥3,000 – ¥5,000 | ¥3,000 – ¥5,000 | Low-cost MVNO SIMs (Ahamo, Rakuten, UQ, Linemo) |
| **National Health Insurance & Pension** | ¥18,000 – ¥25,000 | ¥20,000 – ¥30,000 | Deducted automatically from salary for full-time workers |
| **Transportation** | ¥0 – ¥5,000 | ¥0 – ¥10,000 | Commuting train pass is usually reimbursed by employers |
| **Miscellaneous & Personal Care** | ¥10,000 – ¥15,000 | ¥15,000 – ¥20,000 | Toiletries, clothing, leisure |
| **Total Monthly Expenses** | **¥89,000 – ¥137,000** | **¥133,000 – ¥194,000** | |

*With average SSW salaries between **¥190,000 to ¥260,000**, workers consistently save **¥80,000 to ¥120,000 per month** (approx. 170,000 – 260,000 LKR).*

## 2. Essential Administrative Steps on Arrival

1. **Residence Card (*Zairyu Card - 在留カード*)**:  
   Issued immediately at Tokyo (Narita/Haneda), Osaka (Kansai), or Nagoya (Chubu) airport immigration. You must carry this card on your person at all times by Japanese law.
2. **Resident Registration at City Hall (*Shiyakusho / Kuyakusho*)**:  
   Within 14 days of moving into your apartment, register your residential address on the back of your Zairyu Card to receive your Resident Record (*Juminhyo*).
3. **Opening a Bank Account**:  
   Foreign residents initially open a **Japan Post Bank (*Yucho Ginko - ゆうちょ銀行*)** account, which accepts newcomers without complex credit checks.

## 3. Remitting Money to Sri Lanka

To send earnings safely and legally to your family or bank account in Sri Lanka:
- **Authorized Remittance Services**: Use licensed financial operators such as **Kyodai Remittance**, **SBI Remit**, **Western Union**, or **Brastel Remit**.
- **Tax Benefit on Remittances**: Sending funds to dependent parents in Sri Lanka can qualify you for the **Fuyo控除 (Dependent Tax Deduction)** in Japan, lowering your residence and income tax bills!

## 4. Lump-Sum Pension Refund (脱退一時金 - Nenkin Refund)

All foreign workers contribute to the Japanese National Pension system (*Kousei Nenkin*). When you complete your work tenure and permanently leave Japan:
- You are legally entitled to claim back your paid contributions as a **Lump-Sum Withdrawal Payment (*Dattai Ichijikin*)**.
- For 3 to 5 years of employment, workers typically receive a refund between **¥300,000 to ¥1,000,000+** (approx. 650,000 to 2,000,000+ LKR) deposited into their Sri Lankan bank account.

## 5. Daily Life Norms & Safety

- **Garbage Sorting (*Gomi Bunbetsu*)**: Japan strictly separates Burnable (*Moeru gomi*), Non-burnable (*Moenai gomi*), Plastic bottles (*PET bottle*), Glass, and Cans according to specific colored bags and weekly pickup calendars.
- **Bicycle Registration**: All bicycles must be registered against theft (*Bouhan Touroku*) at local police stations.
- **Emergency Numbers**:
  - **110**: Police (Accidents, theft, emergency protection)
  - **119**: Ambulance & Fire Department
