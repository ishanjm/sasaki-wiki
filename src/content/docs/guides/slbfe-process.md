Under the **Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment Act No. 21 of 1985**, every Sri Lankan citizen departing for overseas employment must be registered with the **SLBFE** prior to departure from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).

**Sasaki Foreign Employment Agency** ensures 100% legal compliance, safeguarding your employment rights, family benefits, and government welfare protections.

---

## Why SLBFE Registration is Mandatory

1. **Airport Immigration Clearance**: Sri Lankan Immigration Officers at CMB verify the SLBFE sticker/digital clearance before stamping exit passports for employment visas.
2. **Comprehensive Insurance Protection**: Provides comprehensive life, disability, critical illness, and repatriation insurance coverage for the worker.
3. **Legal Contract Protection**: Guarantees that the Japanese employer adheres strictly to the salary, accommodation, and working condition terms submitted to the Embassy and Bureau.
4. **Family Welfare & Scholarships**: Dependents of registered workers become eligible for SLBFE welfare schemes, including children's school scholarships and emergency crisis grants.

## Step-by-Step SLBFE Registration Process

<Steps>
1. **Receipt of Japanese COE & Visa Approval**  
   Once the Japanese Immigration Bureau issues your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) and the Japanese Embassy grants the employment visa sticker, your file transitions to the SLBFE liaison division.

2. **Bilingual Employment Agreement Attestation**  
   The employment contract signed between the Japanese employer and the candidate is submitted for formal endorsement to the Embassy of Sri Lanka in Tokyo, Japan, or directly to the SLBFE Special Unit in Colombo.

3. **Mandatory Pre-Departure Training Course**  
   Candidates attend the official SLBFE pre-departure briefing at an authorized training facility. The module covers labor rights overseas, financial literacy, remittance methods, stress management, and consular assistance.

4. **Medical Fitness Endorsement**  
   Submission of the medical clearance certificate issued by an SLBFE-authorized diagnostic medical center.

5. **Biometric Data Capture & Registration Fee**  
   The candidate visits the SLBFE Provincial Office or Head Office in Battaramulla. Digital fingerprints and facial biometrics are captured. The official registration and insurance fee is paid.

6. **Affixing of the Digital SLBFE Clearance**  
   The electronic clearance endorsement is linked to your passport and registered in the Sri Lanka Immigration Emigration Department's border control database.
</Steps>

## Documents Required for Bureau Clearance

- Original Passport (valid for at least 1 year) with stamped Japanese work visa.
- Original and certified copies of the Japanese Employment Agreement / Contract.
- Certificate of Eligibility (COE) copy.
- SLBFE Pre-Departure Training Certificate.
- Medical Fitness Examination Certificate from designated clinic.
- National Identity Card (NIC) and 4 passport-size photographs.
- Nominee / Beneficiary details (for the insurance scheme).
