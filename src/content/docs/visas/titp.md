The **Technical Intern Training Program (TITP / 技能実習 - Ginou Jisshu)** was established to foster international cooperation and transfer technical skills, industrial technology, and knowledge from Japanese enterprises to developing nations.

In 2024, the Japanese Diet passed historic reforms transitioning TITP into the new **"Training and Employment System" (育成就労制度 - Ikusei Shuro)**, strengthening foreign worker rights, establishing clearer career progression into the **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)** system, and facilitating worker retention.

---

## Program Structure & Progression

```
[1st Year: Technical Intern (i) - 技能実習1号]
        │
        ▼ (Pass Basic Trade Skill Test & Practical Exam)
[2nd & 3rd Years: Technical Intern (ii) - 技能実習2号]
        │
        ▼ (Pass Intermediate 3rd Grade Skill Test)
[4th & 5th Years: Technical Intern (iii) - 技能実習3号 (Accredited Organizations)]
        │
        ▼
[Direct Transition to Specified Skilled Worker (SSW (i)) without exam]
```

### Technical Intern Categories
1. **Technical Intern Training (i)**: First year of stay focusing on basic skill acquisition, Japanese language reinforcement, and orientation under supervision.
2. **Technical Intern Training (ii)**: 2nd and 3rd years of stay focusing on practical application. Requires passing the National Basic Trade Skills Examination at the end of Year 1.
3. **Technical Intern Training (iii)**: 4th and 5th years reserved for supervising entities and host enterprises meeting exceptional audit compliance and evaluation scores.

## Key Roles & Operational Framework

The program operates under strict bilateral agreements overseen by the **Organization for Technical Intern Training (OTIT / 外国人技能実習機構)** and the **Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE)**:

- **Sending Organization (Sasaki Foreign Employment Agency)**: Screens candidates, conducts pre-departure Japanese language and etiquette training, arranges medical clearances, and handles departure logistics.
- **Supervising Organization (監理団体 - Kanridantai)**: Non-profit Japanese business cooperative or chamber of commerce that monitors host companies, ensures compliance with labor standards, and audits intern welfare quarterly.
- **Implementing Organization (実習実施者)**: The host Japanese company that employs the trainee, pays legal wages, provides housing, and assigns technical instructors (*Shido-in*).

## Major Industrial Fields

The TITP system covers 88 distinct occupational categories and 161 operational tasks, including:

- **Construction**: Reinforcing bar placement, scaffolding, formwork, carpentry, plastering, interior finishing.
- **Food Manufacturing**: Bread making,惣菜 (prepared meal) processing, marine products processing, meat processing.
- **Textile & Garment**: Women's and children's dressmaking, industrial sewing.
- **Machinery & Metals**: Casting, forging, machining, sheet metal processing, metal plating, electric welding.
- **Agriculture & Livestock**: Cultivation of vegetables, fruit trees, dairy husbandry, egg poultry.
- **Caregiving (Kaigo)**: Specialized hospital and nursing home care with mandatory language milestone supervision.

## Transition to the New "Training and Employment" (育成就労) System

The new **Ikusei Shuro (育成就労)** framework modernizes technical training with several candidate-friendly improvements:

1. **Clear Pathway to Permanent Employment**: Designed explicitly to prepare workers to smoothly transition into the **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW - 特定技能)** framework after 3 years.
2. **Enhanced Job Transfer Rights**: Allows trainees to change employers within the same industry sector after meeting basic service and language criteria (unlike strict restrictions under legacy TITP).
3. **Strict Oversight on Human Rights**: Prohibits unreasonable fees, enforces standard Japanese minimum wage laws, and mandates standard social and health insurance coverage.
