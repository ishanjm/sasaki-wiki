The **Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能 - Tokutei Ginou)** status of residence was established by the Japanese Government to address severe labor shortages by welcoming foreign nationals with certified workplace skills and Japanese language capabilities.

**Sasaki Nihon Academy** and **Sasaki Foreign Employment Agency** provide targeted Japanese language preparation, skill evaluation training, registered employer interviews, and visa processing for Sri Lankan candidates seeking SSW employment in Japan.

---

## SSW Visa Categories

Japan offers two distinct categories under the Specified Skilled Worker framework:

| Feature | SSW (i) - 特定技能1号 | SSW (ii) - 特定技能2号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skill Level** | Operational readiness (considerable degree of knowledge/experience) | Advanced supervisory & expert craftsmanship |
| **Period of Stay** | Up to **5 years** total (renewed every 1 year, 6 months, or 4 months) | **Indefinite renewal** (eligible for permanent residency pathway) |
| **Language Requirement** | JFT-Basic (A2 level) OR JLPT N4 or higher | Advanced workplace Japanese proficiency |
| **Skill Examination** | Industry-specific Skill Evaluation Test | Level 2 / Advanced Technical Skill Test |
| **Family Accompaniment** | Not permitted (except under special humanitarian exceptions) | **Permitted** (spouse and dependent children may accompany) |
| **Support System** | Mandatory support plan via Registered Support Organization (RSO) | Direct employer management (RSO support optional) |

## Approved Employment Sectors

The SSW framework encompasses major industrial fields across Japan:

1. **Nursing Care / Elderly Caregiving (介護)**  
   *Special requirement*: Requires both the Nursing Care Japanese Language Evaluation Test and the Nursing Care Skills Evaluation Test. High demand across modern medical and elder-care facilities.
2. **Food Service Industry (外食業)**  
   Restaurant service, kitchen operations, hygiene management, and culinary customer service.
3. **Food and Beverage Manufacturing (飲食料品製造業)**  
   Food processing, automated packaging, safety control, and factory management.
4. **Agriculture (農業)**  
   Crop farming, greenhouse cultivation, livestock management, harvesting, and sorting.
5. **Construction Industry (建設業)**  
   Formwork, steel reinforcement, scaffolding, interior finishing, earthworks, and heavy machinery operation.
6. **Industrial Machinery & Manufacturing (素形材・産業機械・電気電子情報関連製造業)**  
   Casting, machining, metal pressing, welding, electronic assembly, and factory equipment maintenance.
7. **Accommodation / Hospitality (宿泊業)**  
   Front desk reception, concierge services, hospitality planning, and hotel customer relations.
8. **Automobile Repair & Maintenance (自動車整備業)**  
   Vehicle inspection, chassis maintenance, electronic system diagnosis, and engine repair.
9. **Building Cleaning Management (ビルクリーニング)**  
   Sanitation of commercial premises, hospitals, and high-rise facilities using modern cleaning machinery.

## Eligibility Criteria for Sri Lankan Applicants

To qualify for an SSW (i) visa, candidates must meet the following criteria:

- **Age**: Minimum 18 years old at the time of visa application.
- **Language Proficiency**:
  - **JFT-Basic (Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese)** (Score 200+ out of 250), **OR**
  - **JLPT N4** (or higher) certificate.
  - *For Caregiving*: Additional pass in the Nursing Care Japanese Evaluation Test.
- **Skills Evaluation**: Pass the official Skill Evaluation Test corresponding to your chosen industry sector.
- **TITP Exemption**: Candidates who have successfully completed the Technical Intern Training Program (TITP ii) in Japan in a related field are **exempt from both language and skills tests**.
- **Good Standing**: Clean police record, verified medical fitness, and compliance with Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) registration regulations.

## Rights and Remuneration

Under Japanese Immigration and Labor Law:
- **Equal Pay**: Foreign workers under SSW must receive equal or higher wages compared to Japanese nationals performing equivalent job duties.
- **Labor Protections**: Fully covered by Japanese Labor Standards, Worker's Accident Compensation Insurance, Health Insurance, and National Pension.
- **Freedom to Change Employers**: Unlike traditional internship schemes, SSW holders are legally entitled to transfer employers within the same industrial sector.
- **Comprehensive Support**: Registered Support Organizations (登録支援機関) assist with housing acquisition, bank account setup, airport transfers, Japanese language continuation, and complaint resolution.
