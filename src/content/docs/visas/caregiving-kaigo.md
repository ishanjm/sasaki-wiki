Due to Japan's super-aging society, **Elderly Caregiving (Kaigo - 介護)** has the highest placement quota, exceptional job stability, and comprehensive government support for Sri Lankan candidates under the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) and Technical Intern Training (TITP) programs.

**Sasaki Nihon Academy** conducts targeted nursing care Japanese language preparation and practical caregiving training aligned with Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare standards.

---

## The Two Specialized Exams for Caregiving SSW

Unlike other SSW sectors that require only one general language test and one skill test, Caregiving candidates must pass two distinct components:

1. **Language Component (Must pass one of the following)**:
   - **JFT-Basic (Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese)**: Score 200+ out of 250, **OR**
   - **JLPT N4 or higher**: Official passing certificate.
   - **PLUS**: **Nursing Care Japanese Language Evaluation Test (介護日本語評価試験)**: Computer-based test evaluating specialized medical, anatomical, and caregiving terminology.

2. **Skills Component**:
   - **Nursing Care Skills Evaluation Test (介護技能評価試験)**: Testing practical knowledge in physical care (transfer, mobility, hygiene, dietary assistance, emotional care, dementia care principles).

Both tests are administered via Prometric computer-based testing centers in Colombo and surrounding examination facilities.

---

## Core Workplace Duties in Japanese Care Facilities

Caregivers work in modern elder-care facilities, nursing homes (*Tokuyou*), and day-care centers:

- **Mobility & Transfer Assistance (移乗・移動介助)**: Safely helping elderly residents move between beds, wheelchairs, and dining areas using ergonomic lifting techniques.
- **Dietary & Meal Support (食事介助)**: Monitoring nutrition, preparing specialized textures, assisting residents during meal times, and preventing aspiration.
- **Hygiene & Bathing Care (入浴・排泄介助)**: Assisting with daily personal hygiene, bathing, and dressing while preserving individual dignity (*Songen*).
- **Recreation & Emotional Well-Being (レクリエーション)**: Conducting physical therapy games, cultural activities, and conversational support to maintain cognitive vitality.
- **Observation & Record Keeping (介護記録)**: Logging daily vital signs, food intake, and behavioral changes in digital facility software in Japanese.

## Working Conditions & Remuneration

- **Base Salary**: Typically **¥180,000 to ¥230,000 per month** (approx. 380,000 – 500,000 LKR).
- **Shift Allowances**: Additional allowances for night shifts (*Yakin Teate*) ranging from **¥5,000 to ¥10,000 per night shift**.
- **Working Hours**: Standard 40-hour work week across rotating day, evening, and night shifts with scheduled consecutive off-days.
- **Housing Assistance**: Care facilities frequently provide subsidized furnished staff dormitories near the facility (*Shadaku*), keeping rent low (¥15,000 – ¥30,000/month).

---

## Advancement Pathway to Japanese Certified Caregiver (*Kaigofukushishi*)

After working for 3 years in Japan, candidates are eligible to sit for the national **Kaigofukushishi (介護福祉士)** state examination:
- Passing this exam grants the prestigious **"Nursing Care" (介護)** professional status of residence.
- This status offers **unlimited renewals**, entitlement to bring spouse and children to Japan, and immediate qualification for permanent residency.
