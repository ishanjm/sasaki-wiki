The **Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services (技術・人文知識・国際業務 - Gijinkoku)** status of residence is Japan's primary employment visa category for professional, technical, and white-collar roles.

Unlike blue-collar or technical internship schemes, this visa is intended for university degree holders or individuals with extensive industry experience. It provides high job mobility, career progression, family accompaniment rights, and a direct pathway to permanent residency in Japan.

---

## Three Categories Covered

1. **Engineer (技術 - Gijutsu)**  
   - Fields: Software Engineering, Web Development, Artificial Intelligence, Civil & Mechanical Engineering, Architecture, Electrical Engineering, Biotechnology.
   - Requirements: Bachelor's degree (BSc) in a related discipline or 10+ years of verifiable technical experience.
2. **Specialist in Humanities (人文知識 - Jinbun Chishiki)**  
   - Fields: Accounting, International Finance, Corporate Marketing, Human Resources, Legal Compliance, Supply Chain Management.
   - Requirements: University degree (BA/BSc) in business, economics, social sciences, or law.
3. **International Services (国際業務 - Kokusai Gyoumu)**  
   - Fields: Translation, Interpretation, Language Instruction, International Trade, Public Relations, Overseas Business Liaison.
   - Requirements: University degree or 3+ years of professional experience in the relevant domain.

---

## Key Advantages for Sri Lankan Graduates

- **Standard Employment Contracts**: Employed directly as a permanent (*Seishain*) or contract (*Keiyakushain*) employee with full Japanese salary standards, bonuses (*Bonus*), and annual pay increments.
- **Family Accompaniment**: Visa holders are legally entitled to bring their spouse and children to live in Japan on **Dependent (家族滞在 - Kazoku Taizai)** visas.
- **Path to Permanent Residency**: Eligible to apply for Japan Permanent Residency after 5 to 10 years of continuous residence (or as fast as 1–3 years under the **Highly Skilled Professional - 高度専門職** points system).
- **No Term Limits**: Visa is renewable indefinitely (typically issued for 1, 3, or 5-year periods) as long as employment is maintained.

---

## Eligibility Criteria for Sri Lankan Applicants

1. **Academic Qualifications**:  
   - A recognized Bachelor's Degree (UGC-approved in Sri Lanka or international equivalent), **OR**  
   - A 2-year Diploma from an accredited Japanese Vocational College (*Senmon Gakko*) awarding the **Senmonshi (専門士)** title.
2. **Relevance of Study to Job**:  
   - The job duties specified in the employment contract must directly correlate with the candidate's academic major or professional background.
3. **Japanese Language Ability**:  
   - While immigration law does not mandate a minimum JLPT level for IT engineers, employers typically require **JLPT N3 or N2** for business communication. Non-technical roles (marketing, trade) generally require **JLPT N2 or N1**.
4. **Binding Contract with a Japanese Company**:  
   - A signed employment contract with an enterprise legally registered in Japan providing remuneration equal to or exceeding Japanese native graduates.
