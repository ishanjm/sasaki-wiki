The **Japan Student Visa (留学 - Ryugaku)** is designed for international students seeking to enroll in Japanese Language Institutes, Vocational Colleges (*Senmon Gakko*), Undergraduate Universities, and Graduate Schools in Japan.

For Sri Lankan students, this pathway provides full linguistic and cultural immersion, recognized academic degrees, and a proven springboard into long-term professional employment in Japan.

---

## Educational Pathways in Japan

```
[Japanese Language Institute (1–2 Years)]
               │
               ▼
   ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
   ▼                                               ▼
[Vocational College (Senmon Gakko - 2–4 Years)]    [University / Graduate School (4+ Years)]
   │                                               │
   └───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                           ▼
  [Permanent Full-Time Work Visa (SSW / Engineer / Humanities / Specialist)]
```

1. **Japanese Language School (1 to 2 Years)**:  
   Intensive study to advance from beginner/N5 to advanced N2/N1 level, alongside preparation for the EJU (Examination for Japanese University Admission) or professional job hunting.
2. **Vocational College (*Senmon Gakko*) (2 to 4 Years)**:  
   Specialized professional training in IT, automobile engineering, hospitality management, business administration, graphic design, or culinary arts. Awards the *Diploma (Senmonshi)*, granting eligibility for Japanese work visas.
3. **Universities & Master's Programs**:  
   Undergraduate Bachelor's (4 years) and Master's Degrees (2 years) across engineering, economics, international relations, and science.

## Key Academic & Intake Timelines

Japanese institutions admit students across four annual intakes:

| Intake Period | Typical Course Length | Application Deadline | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **April Intake** | 2 Years (Longest course) | Mid-November (Previous Year) | University & Senmon Gakko aspirants |
| **July Intake** | 1 Year 9 Months | Mid-March (Same Year) | Students with solid N5/N4 foundation |
| **October Intake** | 1 Year 6 Months | Mid-May (Same Year) | Major intake for autumn enrollments |
| **January Intake** | 1 Year 3 Months (Shortest) | Mid-September (Previous Year) | Intermediate students aiming for fast graduation |

## Eligibility Requirements for Sri Lankan Students

To qualify for a Student Visa and obtain the Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from Japanese Immigration:

1. **Formal Education**: Minimum **12 years of completed school education** (G.C.E. Advanced Level / A-Levels completed with passed subjects).
2. **Japanese Language Foundation**:
   - Proof of at least **150 hours of verified Japanese language study** at an accredited academy such as Sasaki Nihon Academy, **OR**
   - Official passing certificate for **JLPT N5** or **NAT-TEST 5Q / JFT-Basic**.
3. **Financial Sponsorship**:
   - A recognized financial sponsor (typically parents, siblings, or direct family members).
   - Verifiable annual income to comfortably support tuition and living expenses.
   - Bank balance certificate verifying funds (typically between **2.5 to 3.0 Million JPY equivalent**, or approx. 6–7 Million LKR) maintained in a legitimate bank account.
   - Proof of tax payment and business registration / employment credentials of the sponsor.
4. **Statement of Purpose (SOP / Reason for Study)**:
   - A clear, cohesive personal essay outlining academic objectives, career goals, and commitment to studying in Japan.

## Part-Time Work Permission (資格外活動許可)

International students on a Student Visa are legally entitled to work part-time in Japan by applying for the **Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted Under the Status of Residence Previously Granted**:

- **Working Hours**: Up to **28 hours per week** during academic semesters.
- **Vacation Hours**: Up to **40 hours per week (8 hours/day)** during official school holidays and summer/winter breaks.
- **Permitted Sectors**: Convenience stores, restaurants, supermarkets, warehouse logistics, language tutoring, and office administration.
- **Prohibited Sectors**: Entertainment establishments, bars, adult entertainment, and gambling venues.
- **Average Hourly Wages**: Ranging between **¥1,050 to ¥1,350 per hour** (varying by prefecture and night shifts), enabling students to comfortably cover their monthly living costs.
